Open-response coding framework

AI-assisted, human-verified: A language model may propose and apply codes, but an analyst checks every assignment against the source response and resolves ambiguous cases.

ThemeInclude whenExclude when
Visible resultsMentions demonstrations, before/after evidence or observed performance.General popularity without product evidence.
Trusted reviewMentions detailed creator, peer or verified-buyer experience.Celebrity awareness without evaluation.
Ingredient clarityRequests understandable ingredients, benefits or suitability.Packaging aesthetics alone.
Talent-product fitEvaluates whether the spokesperson credibly connects to the category or benefit.Reach, fame or fandom alone.
Local suitabilityMentions climate, skin concerns, price, retail availability or routine fit in Singapore.General Korean provenance.

Each response can receive multiple codes. AI output must preserve respondent IDs and supporting excerpts. Counts are produced only after human review and de-duplication.
