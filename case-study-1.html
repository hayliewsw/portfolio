Overview

I designed and built a personal inventory management system by converting a CSV database into a user-friendly website. The goal was to make stock, sales, and item movement easier to organize and review.

The challenge

A spreadsheet held the information, but it did not make the work easy to understand.

Tracking availability, item categories, sales, and movement across a raw CSV required too much manual scanning. The project focused on making records easier to find, interpret, and maintain through a simple digital interface.

Design decisions
01

Organize around real tasks

The system groups records around the actions that matter: checking availability, reviewing movement, and keeping listings current.

02

Make status scannable

Clear categories and inventory states reduce the effort required to understand what needs attention.

03

Keep the source maintainable

A structured interface supports accurate documentation while preserving the practical value of the underlying data.

Final direction

Operational information made easier to act on.

The result translates a raw database into an intuitive inventory interface that supports listing, storage, fulfillment, and record keeping.
