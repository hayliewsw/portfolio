Abstract

How Singapore consumers discover, try and buy K-beauty—based on a SYRES online survey of 4,800+ adults across France and Singapore. Key signal: price drives trial; performance builds appeal.

01

Introduction

From cultural attention to product choice.

K-beauty is now widely used in Singapore. This study tracks what drives awareness, trial, purchase and satisfaction.

02

Methodology

Collected online.
Verified against source.

Survey

20+ closed and open-ended questions.

Participants

4,800+ adults aged 20–65 across France and Singapore.

Timing

July 2024. This page focuses on Singapore.

Process

Extract → structure with AI → verify → present. AI did not collect the data.

03

Most Singapore respondents had tried K-beauty. Price, reviews and recommendations mattered most.

03

The central tension

Social creates awareness.
Results create appeal.

Our interpretation of SYRES findings—not a direct study quote.

Awareness

Social leads discovery.

84% heard about K-beauty through social networks. 58% cited friends or family.

Overall product appeal

Results define the category.

In a separate question about K-beauty’s single most attractive feature, 45% chose effectiveness, versus price at 22% and ingredient quality at 12%.

05

Additional Findings

Six signals shaping the category.

Product mix · Report p.11

Skincare anchors the category.

Skincare 96% · Masks 80% · Cleansers 72% · Makeup 71%

Frequency · Report p.13

Purchasing is often occasional.

58% purchased K-beauty occasionally, while 36% did so regularly.

Interpretation

There is an opportunity to turn experimentation into routine use.

Influence · Report pp.22, 25

Social creates awareness, not conviction.

84% discovered via social. Influence: 30% “a lot” · 63% “a little.”

Value perception · Report pp.15–16

Satisfaction ≠ differentiation.

81% are satisfied. Only 49% see more benefits.

Interpretation

Make the difference explicit.

Associations · Report p.19

Culture shapes the image.

Beauty or glamour 69% · Trendy 67%

Culture · Report pp.23–24

Culture reaches beyond K-dramas.

Cultural trends influence trial: 77% · Korean series increase interest: 43%
