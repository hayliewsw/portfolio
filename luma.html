Overview

Oupelaye Studio is a game studio I co-founded in Quebec. I am responsible for art direction and UX, working closely with development to shape the game’s visual world and player experience.

The opportunity

Games are products where every interaction shapes how a player feels and understands.

My work connects visual direction with game UX: considering how a player encounters information, receives feedback, and moves through the experience while keeping the world coherent and engaging.
