Overview

Luma is an original indie game experience built around exploration, discovery, and small moments of wonder. I used product design methods to shape the core loop, user interface, and systems that guide a player without interrupting the feeling of discovery.

The challenge

How might we teach a new world without taking away the joy of figuring it out?

The central tension in Luma was onboarding. Players needed enough feedback to understand the world and its objectives—but not so much instruction that the experience lost its sense of curiosity.

Design decisions
01

Teach through the environment

Landmarks, lighting, and spatial cues help players understand where to go before a written instruction is necessary.

02

Reward the next question

A simple progression loop uses immediate feedback and long-term mystery to give exploration an inviting rhythm.

03

Keep the UI out of the way

Contextual guidance appears only when it helps a player act, keeping attention rooted in the world rather than the interface.

Final direction

A system designed for curiosity.

Soft visual feedback, calm pacing, and lightweight goals create room for the player to discover their own path while always feeling gently supported.
