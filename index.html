I make space for people in product decisions.

I’m a product designer with four years of dedicated UX/UI and digital product experience across Singapore, now based in Toronto. My work sits at the intersection of information, workflows, and visual craft.

As co-founder of Oupelaye Studio, I also shape game UX and art direction in close collaboration with development. My process is practical: understand the work, make the path clear, and refine it with the people building and using it.
