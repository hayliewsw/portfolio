(02) About me

I make space for people in product decisions.

For the last five years, I’ve designed digital products at the intersection of strategy, systems, and visual craft. I also make indie games—which has made me especially curious about feedback loops, emotion, and the small details that make an interaction feel alive.

My process is practical and collaborative: understand the problem, make the path clear, prototype early, and keep improving with the people building and using it.
