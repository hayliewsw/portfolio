Overview

Aurora Travel is a concept for a calmer, more transparent way to plan a trip. The experience brings itinerary decisions, price context, and passenger needs into one focused booking flow.

The challenge

Travel booking often asks people to make important choices without enough clarity.

When options, fees, and trip details compete for attention, planning can feel more stressful than exciting. The opportunity was to create a lighter decision-making experience without hiding the details travellers need.

Design decisions
01

Keep the next step obvious

A progressive flow places only the most relevant choice in front of the traveller, with trip context carried forward throughout.

02

Make price feel explainable

Fare, baggage, and timing information is grouped into clear comparison moments so customers can choose with confidence.

03

Design for the whole journey

A living itinerary creates a reassuring home for booking details before, during, and after the flight.

Final direction

Helpful details, delivered at the right moment.

The visual direction balances warmth with clarity: spacious layouts, calm color, and just enough feedback to make each choice feel understood.
